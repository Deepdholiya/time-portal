How does pricing work?+

We set each team up personally. Request access with your work email and we'll reply with a quote and your workspace.

What happens after I request access?+

The Orbit0 team creates your company workspace and sends you an invite. You then invite your team from the portal.

Can I try it first?+

Yes. The live demo has two sample companies and one-click sign-ins for Admin, Manager and Employee.

Can one person belong to more than one company?+

Yes. One login can belong to several companies, and each company's data stays its own.
