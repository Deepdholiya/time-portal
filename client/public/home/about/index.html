Why we built it

Hours were the one thing
nobody could see.

Teams that bill by the project live and die by their hours, yet most still log them in a spreadsheet on Friday afternoon, from memory. Leave sits in another tool, expenses in a third, and the budget overrun shows up on invoice day.

We built Orbit0 so the hours go in quickly and every one of them leads somewhere: a project, a budget, a report, a client update. Time, projects, leave and approvals share one set of people and permissions, so the numbers agree with each other.

The name comes from the idea at its centre. Every hour your team works orbits a project, and Orbit0 keeps them all in view.

Principles

What we believe.

Logging should take seconds

If it takes longer, people do it on Friday from memory. So we made a duration and a project enough.

Every hour should lead somewhere

Time that never reaches a report or a budget is wasted twice. Orbit0 connects it all.

You stay in control

Admins decide who sees what. AI drafts, and nothing is sent until a person presses send.
