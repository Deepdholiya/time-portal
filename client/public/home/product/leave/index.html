Three steps

How it works.

  1. 1Request it

    Pick vacation, sick, personal or other, the dates and a half day if needed.

  2. 2Manager approves

    Requests wait in the manager's Approvals queue with everything they need.

  3. 3It shows everywhere

    The days appear on calendars and timesheets, and reminders skip them.
