01 · Time tracking

Log a day in a minute.

Type a duration, pick the project and say what you did. The start fills itself from where your last entry ended, so nobody runs a timer all day.

  • Start time fills from your last entry
  • Overlaps and future dates are blocked
  • Each day submits itself at your cutoff
  • Tags, required where you need them

  • Leave, holidays, tasks and milestones as layers
  • Team timesheets grouped any way you like
  • Lock days a manager approved

Plan the work where the hours land.

  • Tasks in list or board view, with dependencies
  • Roadmap built from project dates and milestones
  • A read-only status link for your client

05 · Leave and approvals

Leave and expenses in one inbox.

Leave and reimbursement requests land in one Approvals queue. Approved leave shows on the calendar and blocks those days for logging.

  • Leave requests and company holidays
  • Reimbursements with receipts
  • One Approvals queue for managers
  • Every change kept in the audit log

06 · Ask AI

Ask about your time in plain words.

Ask from the search bar and get an answer with the numbers behind it. AI also tidies descriptions, drafts tasks and summaries, and drafts client updates you review.

  • Answers only with what your role can see
  • Drafts tasks and project summaries
  • Drafts client emails, sent only when you press send
  • A copy of every email in the email log

07 · Admin

You decide who sees what.

Start with Admin, Manager and Employee, add your own roles, and override a single person when you need to. Run more than one company from one login.

  • Custom roles and per-person overrides
  • Several companies, each with its own data and currency
  • Brand colour per company
  • Google or Microsoft sign-in per company
