Three steps

How it works.

  1. 1Type a duration

    Enter 1:30 or 90m. Orbit0 starts it where your last entry ended, or at your work start.

  2. 2Pick the project

    Choose the project, sub-project or task and add a short description and tags.

  3. 3Your day submits itself

    At your company's cutoff, 23:59 by default, the day goes in for approval.

What you get

Fast for the team,
clean for the reports.

Start fills itself

Each entry starts where the last one ended, or at your work start time.

Overlaps blocked

Two entries can't cover the same minutes, and future dates are rejected.

Log against tasks

Pick a task and see the time add up against its estimate.

Tags where you need them

Make tags required, and limit which teams see which tags.

Daily cutoff

Days submit themselves at the cutoff, so nobody forgets on Friday.

Approved days locked

Once a manager approves a day, it can't be changed by accident.
